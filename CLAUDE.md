## Elias's fork

This is Elias's fork of OpenBot ([docs/fork/README.md](docs/fork/README.md)). **Every fork change adds or
updates its entry in [docs/fork/FEATURES.md](docs/fork/FEATURES.md) in the same commit.** The Dock
launcher (`scripts/fork/app.sh`) builds and installs `main`; do not install builds by hand.

When Elias mentions a gap that a skipped feature in [docs/fork/LATER.md](docs/fork/LATER.md) would close
(phone away from home, ChatGPT/Claude app → bots, bots on another Mac, messages lost while the Mac
sleeps, fallback chain…), point to LATER.md and offer the entry.
